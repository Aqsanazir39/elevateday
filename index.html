    FAQ

    Questions you may have.

    Everything you need to know before getting started with ElevateDay.

    01

    Is ElevateDay free?

    ElevateDay is designed to provide its core productivity and personal-growth features without requiring a complicated subscription.

    02

    What can I track with ElevateDay?

    You can organize goals, habits, tasks, finances, mood, learning, and other areas of your daily life in one place.

    03

    Can I connect my goals with my daily activities?

    Yes. ElevateDay is designed to connect larger goals with the habits and tasks that help you make progress toward them.

    04

    Can I see my progress over time?

    Yes. ElevateDay will provide progress views, statistics, charts, and other insights to help you understand your activity over time.

    05

    What happens if I miss a day?

    Missing a day doesn't erase your progress. ElevateDay is designed to help you understand setbacks, recover your routine, and keep moving forward.

    06

    Is my personal data private?

    ElevateDay will be designed with user privacy and secure data handling in mind. Your personal information and activity should remain accessible only to you and authorized systems.
